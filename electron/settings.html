Copy a job. Press a key. Paste a winning proposal.

How to use

  1. Select the job posting with your mouse (tip: Ctrl+A selects the whole page).
  2. Press Ctrl+Shift+1. A message pops up when it is ready.
  3. Click the proposal box and paste (Ctrl+V).

Ctrl+Shift+1 is taken by another app. Use the button below or the tray menu.
