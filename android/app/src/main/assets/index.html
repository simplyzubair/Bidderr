Turn on the floating B bubble

The green B floats over job pages in Chrome and the Upwork app. Tap it and Bidderr reads the job by itself. No selecting, no copying.

  1. Tap Open settings below.
  2. Find Bidderr bubble (maybe under "Installed apps") and turn it on.
  3. If it is greyed out: go to Settings, Apps, Bidderr, tap the ⋮ menu (top right), then Allow restricted settings. Then try step 2 again.
